## Academic Reading and Writing

- [How to Read a Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/1273445.1273458) by Prof. Srinivasan Keshav - Describes a three-stage strategy for systematically reading research papers.
- [How to Read a Technical Paper](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~jason/advice/how-to-read-a-paper.html) by Prof. Jason Eisner - Practical strategies for reading and analyzing technical research papers.
- [Write the Paper First](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~jason/advice/write-the-paper-first.html) by Prof. Jason Eisner - Advice on structuring research by drafting the paper early.
- A Writer’s Reference — Diana Hacker and Nancy Sommers - A comprehensive handbook for developing professional and academic writing skills.
- On Writing Well — William Zinsser - A classic guide to nonfiction writing. Although nearly 50 years old, it remains an excellent resource for understanding writing principles (Part I) and methods (Part II). The author also discusses different forms of writing in Part III. As William Zinsser notes, “Learning is a tonic.”
- [Writing Tips](https://users.umiacs.umd.edu/~ying/static/style.html) by Prof. Jordan Boyd-Graber - Practical guidance on academic writing and research communication.
- [How to Write a Research Paper](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~poole/HowToWriteResearchPaper.html) by Prof. David Poole - Practical advice on structuring and writing a clear research paper.

## Style Guides and Publication Standards

- [Awesome ML Conference Paper Writing Style Guide](https://github.com/smsnobin77/awesome-ML-conference-paper-writing-style-guide) by Nobin Sarwar - A practical resource outlining structure, clarity, and stylistic conventions for high-quality ML research papers.
- [IEEE Reference Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1j1L96U2NagwWI9MEVDNVKt9pXxRzTH7h3krI3Mb6wZE/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.b2e0set9htjw) - Provides essential guidance on citations, abbreviations, formatting, and scholarly writing standards. [IEEE Author Center source](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/your-role-in-article-production/ieee-editorial-style-manual/)
- [IEEE Editorial Style Manual for Authors](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OalFYqVfxKKMelF79U3B93SjmIEiTnJC12wPJrKeSDM/edit?tab=t.0) - A detailed manual covering editorial conventions and best practices for technical writing. [IEEE Author Center source](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/your-role-in-article-production/ieee-editorial-style-manual/)
- How to cite properly - Different takes on doing citations right: [Jordan Boyd-Graber](https://users.umiacs.umd.edu/~ying/static/style.html) on citation style · [John Owens](https://www.ece.ucdavis.edu/~jowens/biberrors.html) on common BibTeX errors · [Jia-Bin Huang](https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1672342931473137664) on citation etiquette · [IEEE Citation Guidelines](https://ieee-dataport.org/sites/default/files/analysis/27/IEEE%20Citation%20Guidelines.pdf) for formal reference

## Research Communication

- [How to Speak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Unzc731iCUY) — Prof. Patrick Winston (YouTube) - A widely recommended lecture on structuring and delivering research presentations.
- [How to Ask a Question in Class](https://www.quora.com/Do-professors-enjoy-answering-high-level-questions-more-than-low-level-ones/answer/Jason-Eisner?share=1) by Prof. Jason Eisner - Insightful advice on asking thoughtful, high-level academic questions.
- [Revising an Academic Poster](https://x.com/jbhuang0604/status/1974198882906325054) — Prof. Jia-Bin Huang (Twitter Thread) - Practical guidance on improving research posters for clarity and impact.
- [Generic Conference Talk Outline](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~markhill/conference-talk.html) - Prof. Mark Hill; [How to Give a Bad Talk](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~markhill/conference-talk.html) - Prof. David Patterson

## Research Skills and Mentorship

- [Being a Researcher](https://www.coursera.org/learn/being-researcher) — Prof. Carlo Ghezzi, Politecnico di Milano (Coursera) - A reflective course on research mindset and academic professionalism.
- [Understanding Research Methods](https://www.coursera.org/learn/research-methods) — SOAS University of London (Coursera) - An introductory course covering core research methodology concepts.
- [Awesome Academic NLP Research Labs Worldwide](https://github.com/smsnobin77/awesome-academic-nlp-labs) by Nobin Sarwar - A curated directory of academic NLP research labs worldwide.
- [NLP PhD Global Equality](https://github.com/zhijing-jin/nlp-phd-global-equality) by Zhijing Jin - Open resources and information to help people succeed in a CS PhD and a career in AI/NLP.
- [CS PhD Application Fee Waivers](https://github.com/KaiserWhoLearns/CS-PhD-Application-fee-waivers) by Kaiser Sun - A collection of CS PhD application fee waivers offered by schools in North America.
- [Advice](https://github.com/shaily99/advice) by Shaily Bhatt - A curated set of links with advice on grad school applications, research, and the PhD.
- [CS Graduate Applications](https://github.com/chinasaokolo/csGraduateApps) by Chinasa T. Okolo - A collection of graduate application materials for prospective CS master's and PhD students.
- [Academic Research Opportunities for International Students](https://github.com/smsnobin77/Academic-research-opportunities-for-international-students) by Nobin Sarwar - A curated list of research mentorship programs for international students.
- [Awesome ML Summer Schools (MLSS)](https://github.com/awesome-mlss/awesome-mlss) by the community and Nobin Sarwar - A curated collection of machine learning summer schools worldwide.

## Online Courses and Specializations

- [Machine Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/machine-learning-introduction) — DeepLearning.AI and Stanford (Coursera) - Core ML: supervised and unsupervised learning.
- [Deep Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/deep-learning) — DeepLearning.AI (Coursera) - Neural networks, CNNs, and sequence models.
- [Natural Language Processing Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/natural-language-processing) — DeepLearning.AI (Coursera) - Core NLP models, from classification to attention.
- [Reinforcement Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/reinforcement-learning) — University of Alberta (Coursera) - RL fundamentals, from MDPs to policy gradients.
- [Generative AI with Large Language Models](https://www.coursera.org/learn/generative-ai-with-llms) — DeepLearning.AI and AWS (Coursera) - LLM pre-training, fine-tuning, and RLHF.
- [Generative AI for Software Development](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/generative-ai-for-software-development) — DeepLearning.AI (Coursera) - LLMs for coding, testing, and software design.
